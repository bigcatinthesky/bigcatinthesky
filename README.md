# Parker Bowman

## My projects

+ ### Entropy
  Entropy is a hard science fiction space exploration RPG developed in the Unity engine and scripted in C# with custom Blender assets. Featuring a wide range of vehicles, locations, and activities across the stranded Terminus system. You play as Elise, a cargo pilot stuck on a decaying station years after the connection between Terminus and the Solar system was severed. When your new home is raised, you must undertake a journey to the system's furthest edges and deepest depths. Find those responsible, and follow the traces of someone you lost long ago. 

+ ### Helldivers II Companion App
  A local web or CLI companion app for the live service game "Helldivers 2", displaying real time live game information in a readable and searchable format. Planets can be searched individually or by galactic sectors. This is a fan-made project, not associated or endorsed by the developers in any way.
